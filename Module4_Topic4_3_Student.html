Spectator Ions — What Can Be Canceled?

Learning Objective: Identify spectator ions as ions unchanged on both sides of a complete ionic equation.

Reactant Side

Meaning of the highlight: the circled ions are spectator ions. They are unchanged in formula, charge, physical state, and coefficient amount, so they can be canceled when writing the net ionic equation.
Na⁺(aq) and NO₃⁻(aq) are spectators in AgNO₃(aq) + NaCl(aq).
Do not cancel species just because they contain the same element. Ag⁺(aq) and AgCl(s) are not the same species.

Complete Cancellation — When Nothing Changes

Learning Objective: Explain why a complete ionic equation can cancel completely when all ions are spectators.
Molecular prediction2KNO₃(aq) + (NH₄)₂SO₄(aq) → K₂SO₄(aq) + 2NH₄NO₃(aq)
All products are aqueous: every ion remains dissolved.
Complete ionic2K⁺ + 2NO₃⁻ + 2NH₄⁺ + SO₄²⁻ → 2K⁺ + SO₄²⁻ + 2NH₄⁺ + 2NO₃⁻
First check: this molecular prediction must be balanced before the ionic equation is trusted.
After proper balancing, all ions cancel → no net ionic reaction.
Interpretation: the beaker still contains the same dissolved ions; no precipitate, gas, or weak electrolyte forms.
