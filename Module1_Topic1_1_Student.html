Slide 4 • Principles

Scientific Law versus Theory

SCIENTIFIC LAW

A concise statement or mathematical equation that [_______]summarizes a vast body of experimental observations.

Key Function: Describes WHAT happens under specific natural conditions.
Example: Law of Conservation of Mass.
SCIENTIFIC THEORY

A well-substantiated, comprehensive, testable [_______]explanation of why a natural phenomenon occurs.

Key Function: Explains WHY it happens at a fundamental level.
Example: Dalton's Atomic Theory.
Key Insight: Laws state what happens; Theories explain why it happens.

Slide 9 • Pitfalls & Corrections

Common Student Mistakes — T1.1

Mistake 1: Believing a Theory eventually turns into a Law.

Correction: Laws describe [_______]what happens (observations), while Theories explain [_______]why (underlying cause). They remain distinct!

Mistake 2: Confusing Compounds with Homogeneous Mixtures.

Correction: Compounds have fixed, definite chemical ratios (e.g. H₂O). Mixtures can vary in [_______]composition (e.g. weak vs strong saltwater).

Topic Summary — T1.1

1. Scope & Domains

Chemistry connects the Macroscopic world we see to the Microscopic realm of atoms through Symbolic notation.
