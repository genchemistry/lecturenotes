Atomic Theory — The Atomic Idea and Dalton’s Model

Learning Objective: Explain the central claims of atomic theory and identify the Dalton statement modified by isotope evidence.

Atom: The smallest unit of an element that retains the element’s chemical identity.

• Matter appears continuous macroscopically but consists of discrete [ _______ ] at the particle level.

1. Elements & Atoms

Elements consist of atoms. Compounds form when atoms combine in simple [ _______ ]-number ratios.

2. Chemical Reactions

Chemical reactions [ _______ ] atoms rather than creating or destroying them.

⚠️
Historical Model → Modern Correction:

Dalton stated all atoms of a given element are identical in mass. Modern Correction: Atoms of the same element can have different masses because isotopes exist.
