56.0% desired CaO

Interpretation: About 56% of the reactant mass becomes the desired CaO. The remaining mass becomes CO₂.
Do not use actual yield. Atom economy comes from the balanced equation and molar masses.

High vs. Low Atom Economy — Quick Check

Learning Objective: Predict whether a reaction has high or low atom economy by looking for byproducts.

High Atom Economy

Most reactant atoms enter the desired product.

Few or no byproducts.

Low Atom Economy

A significant fraction of reactant atoms becomes byproducts.

More waste is generated.

2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O
One desired product and no byproduct: all reactant atoms enter H₂O, so atom economy is 100%.
CaCO₃ → CaO + CO₂
Desired CaO plus CO₂ byproduct: some reactant mass does not enter the desired product, so atom economy is below 100%.
Important: High atom economy does not guarantee high percent yield.
